Black Magic: How 2 Keep a Black Car Black

Owning a black car is like owning a giant mirror that LOVES dust. Here's what I learned the hard way:

  • Wash in the shade. Hot sun dries the soap into spots faster than you can say "Netscape Navigator."
  • Use two buckets. One for soapy water, one for plain water to rinse your mitt. Otherwise you're just rubbing grit all over the paint.
  • Microfiber ONLY. Old T-shirts and paper towels leave teeny swirl marks, and on black paint every swirl shows up like a spiderweb.
  • Dry her right away with a big soft towel. Letting her air dry = water spots city.
  • Wax every couple of months. The rain beads up and rolls right off and it looks AWESOME.

Do all that and she'll shine so bright you can check your hair in the door. Trust me, I do it every morning.
